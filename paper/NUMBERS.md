Values are not fitted or replaced using later observations. Commas are thousands separators; retained precision follows the released claims or cited tables.

## Coverage

- 297 distinct source-mapped empirical values or table cells occur at 332 TeX locations.
- 311 registry entries are available; unused entries are not claims in the paper.
- 4 figure source/data mappings are verified. The scorecard and short-context PDFs are byte-identical to the release; GPU-bandwidth legend wording and throughput-panel layout/legend are presentation changes, with unchanged data and predictions.
- No unclassified numeric literal remains in manuscript TeX after the metadata and layout categories below.
- Regenerate the macros and concrete source selectors with `python3 paper/scripts/generate_evidence.py`. `python3 paper/scripts/audit_numbers.py` checks provenance, coverage and literal classification; it does not recompute every statistic.
